Contract Lifecycle Management

What This Does

Contract Lifecycle Management uses AI to track and manage contracts through their full lifecycle. Upload or paste a contract and the AI extracts key dates, obligations, renewal terms, and generates a lifecycle summary with upcoming actions. Get a single view of renewals, deadlines, and compliance tasks so you never miss a critical date.

Extracts key dates and obligations from contracts; triggers renewals and compliance tasks; supports renegotiation and termination decisions. Ideal for legal ops, business owners, and general counsel who need portfolio visibility.

Example User Journey

1
Upload or Paste

Upload a contract (TXT, PDF, DOCX) or paste the text directly into the input area.

2
AI Extracts Lifecycle Data

The system extracts text, sends it to the Nutanix AI endpoint, and receives a structured lifecycle analysis.

3
Review Lifecycle Summary

See lifecycle status, key dates timeline, obligations tracker, renewal alerts, and action items in one view.

4
Act on Alerts

Use the action items and renewal alerts to plan renegotiations, send notices, or complete obligations on time.
