9.5 Customer Termination. Customer may not terminate this Agreement for convenience during the Initial Term. 9.6 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, all rights granted to Customer shall immediately cease. Sections 3 (to the extent of unpaid Fees), 4.2, 5, 6.2, 7, 8.2, 10, and 11 shall survive termination.
