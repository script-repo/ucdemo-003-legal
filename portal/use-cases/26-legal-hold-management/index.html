Legal Hold Management

What This Does

Legal Hold Management uses AI to draft legal hold notices, generate custodian lists, and create preservation plans. Enter matter details, custodian names, data sources, hold reason, and scope. The system produces a professional hold notice, structured custodian table, preservation plan with methods and deadlines, and a reminder schedule for follow-up.

This tool reduces administrative burden on litigation teams, ensures consistent holds, and maintains a defensible audit trail for court and opposing counsel.

Example User Journey

1
Enter Matter Details

Litigation counsel enters the matter name, matter type, custodian names (one per line), data sources, hold reason, and scope description.

2
AI Generates

The system sends the form data to the Nutanix AI endpoint, which drafts a legal hold notice, structures the custodian list, and creates a preservation plan with deadlines.

3
Review Results

The hold notice appears as formatted text. A custodian table shows name, department, data sources, and status. The preservation plan lists data sources, locations, methods, responsible parties, and deadlines. A reminder schedule suggests follow-up dates and actions.

4
Issue and Track

Counsel reviews, edits if needed, issues the hold to custodians, and uses the reminder schedule for acknowledgments and follow-up.

Data Flow Summary

  1. User enters matter name, matter type, custodian names, data sources, hold reason, and scope via the form.
  2. The browser sends the form data to the API Proxy.
  3. The API Proxy forwards the request (with authentication) to the NAI chat completions endpoint.
  4. NAI generates a structured JSON response with hold notice, custodians, preservation plan, reminder schedule, and risk assessment.
  5. The Portal UI renders the results: hold notice, custodian table, preservation plan, reminder schedule.
  6. Hold metadata is saved to PostgreSQL for audit and history tracking.
