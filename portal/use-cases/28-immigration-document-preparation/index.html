Immigration Document Preparation

What This Does

Immigration Document Preparation uses AI to assist with visa petition preparation. Enter the visa type, beneficiary and petitioner information, job details, and special qualifications. The AI generates a petition outline, a required documents checklist, supporting evidence recommendations, processing timeline, and important warnings to help you assemble a complete, well-structured petition package.

Example User Journey

1
Complete the Form

Select the visa type (H-1B, L-1, O-1, EB-1, EB-2 NIW, PERM, TN, or E-2), enter beneficiary and petitioner details, job description, and special qualifications.

2
AI Generates Outline

The system sends your input to the Nutanix AI inference endpoint and receives a structured petition outline, document checklist, and evidence recommendations.

3
Review Results

Review the petition outline, required documents table, evidence recommendations with strength levels, processing timeline, and any warnings before assembling your package.
